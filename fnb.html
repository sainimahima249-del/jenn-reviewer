🍽️ FOOD AND BEVERAGE SECTOR — EXAM REVIEWER 🍽️

The Food and Beverage (F&B) sector is the largest segment of the hospitality industry, covering everything from star restaurants and catering services to bars and F&B providers for hotels, resorts, and airlines.

Fine Dining features a formal dress code, high-end decor, attentive staff, exotic dishes, and often a sommelier (wine and food pairing expert).

Casual Dining offers moderately-priced menus with table service, low-key atmosphere, and unique decor.

Contemporary Casual also uses table service but focuses on a trendy, "Instagram-worthy" presentation.

Fast Casual uses counter service in a casual, contemporary setting.

Fast Food emphasizes quick service, drive-thru, standardized processed food, and disposable packaging.

A Cafe serves coffee, tea, and pastries in a relaxed, social atmosphere.

Buffet restaurants offer food at a fixed price with self-service.

Food Trucks/Concession Stands serve a small, single-type menu (like hot dogs or ice cream) at outdoor events, using partially or fully pre-made food.

Pop-Up restaurants operate temporarily in varied locations and rely on social media promotion.

Ghost Restaurants are delivery-only with no physical location, operating through apps like UberEats or Grubhub and relying on a strong online presence.

Restaurants are categorized by three main factors: Formality Spectrum (based on decor and expected guest dress, ranging from fine-dining to casual), Price (ranging from inexpensive to pricey), and Food — which includes type of food, quality of ingredients, and presentation of meals.

Restaurant operations refer to all components of the daily management of a restaurant — the processes staff perform to keep it running and meet customer needs. The five key components are: Purchasing, Pricing, Preparation, Service, and Clean-up.
